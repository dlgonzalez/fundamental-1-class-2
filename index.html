Repeating Patterns

After 20, the numbers repeat! You just say the "Ten" number, and add the single digit. (e.g., Thirty + two = 32. Forty + five = 45).

Zero (0) vs. Oh

For ages or quantities, we say "Zero". But for telephone numbers, room numbers, or years, we usually say the letter "Oh".

Survival Phrases

What happens when you don't understand the teacher? In English class, try to ask for help in English! Here are the 5 most important survival phrases.

The Problem ❌ The Survival Phrase 🆘
You don't understand the concept. I don't understand.
You don't know how to write a word. How do you spell that?
You don't know the definition. What does [word] mean?
You need to use the bathroom. May I go to the restroom, please?
The audio is too quiet. Can you speak louder, please?
The teacher is talking but you don't hear anything. You are on mute. / Please turn on your mic.
Your internet is failing and you might disconnect. My internet is unstable. / I have a bad connection.
You need to see the book on the screen. Can you share your screen, please?

Activity 12: Role-Play

The Hotel Check-In

Work with a partner. One is the receptionist, one is the guest. You must use greetings, personal info, spelling, numbers, and at least one survival phrase!

Receptionist

  • Greet the guest.
  • Ask for their first and last name.
  • Ask them to spell their last name.
  • Ask for their phone number.
  • Give them a room number.

Guest

  • Greet the receptionist.
  • Give your real name.
  • Spell your last name clearly.
  • Give a fake phone number and a fake reservation number.
  • Use "Can you repeat that?" at least once.

Useful Language

"What is your reservation number?"
"My reservation number is..."
"Your room number is..."
"Can you spell that, please?"
👀 Show Sample Script

R: Good afternoon! Welcome to the hotel.

G: Hello. I have a reservation.

R: What is your reservation number?

G: It is 8-4-0-2-9.

R: And what is your name, please?

G: My name is Maria Gomez.

R: Can you spell your last name, please?

G: Yes. G - O - M - E - Z.

R: Thank you. And what is your phone number?

G: Can you repeat that, please?

R: What is your phone number?

G: It is 3-1-4-5-5-2-9.

R: Perfect. Your room number is 14.
